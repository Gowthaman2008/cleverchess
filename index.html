📖 How To Play
♟ PIECES & THEIR MOVES
♔
King
Moves 1 square any direction. Must be protected!
♕
Queen
Moves any squares in any direction. Most powerful!
♖
Rook
Moves any squares horizontally or vertically.
♗
Bishop
Moves any squares diagonally. Stays on one color.
♘
Knight
Moves in L-shape. Can jump over pieces!
♙
Pawn
Moves forward 1 (or 2 from start). Captures diagonally.
🎯 OBJECTIVE

Checkmate your opponent's King. Check = King is under attack. Checkmate = King is in check and cannot escape. Stalemate = No legal moves (draw).

✨ SPECIAL MOVES
🏰 Castling — King moves 2 squares toward rook (if neither has moved)
⬆️ En Passant — Pawn captures an adjacent pawn that just moved 2 squares
👑 Promotion — Pawn reaching the last rank becomes any piece (usually Queen)
